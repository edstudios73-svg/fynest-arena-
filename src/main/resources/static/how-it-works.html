How SmartAttend works

Registering for the first time

  1. Enter your index number

    We look it up in the department's verified class register and show the name on record.

  2. Confirm your name

    If the name is wrong, report it — an administrator corrects the register. You can't register under someone else's name.

  3. Agree to the face check

    Read the privacy notice. If you don't consent, choose supervised verification instead.

  4. Enrol your face

    Follow the on-screen head turns in good light. We keep an encrypted numeric template, not your photo.

  5. Create a passkey

    Your phone asks for your fingerprint, Face ID or screen lock. That passkey becomes your sign-in.

Marking attendance

When your lecturer opens attendance, it appears at the top of your SmartAttend home screen. Scan the QR code or type the six-digit code, complete the face check and confirm with your phone's lock. You'll see a "Present" receipt with a reference number once the record is saved. If you arrive after the lecturer's late cut-off, you're recorded as "Late".

Changed or lost your phone?

Open account recovery on the new phone. After a face check, an administrator confirms your identity (for example by checking your student ID in person) and approves the new phone. Your old phone is signed out.

Two kinds of biometric check

The face check runs on SmartAttend's server and compares your live face with your enrolled template. The passkey uses your phone's own fingerprint or Face ID; that biometric never leaves your phone and SmartAttend can't see it. A web browser can't read your phone's Face ID data, which is why both layers exist.

What counts towards your percentage

Attendance % = attended sessions ÷ eligible sessions × 100. Only closed sessions count. Late counts as attended and excused sessions are left out, unless the department changes these policies. Records waiting for verification count once a lecturer approves them.
