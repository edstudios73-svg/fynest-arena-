Marking attendance takes four steps

  1. Your lecturer opens attendance

    A six-digit code and QR code appear on the classroom screen. Both change every minute.

  2. You scan the QR or type the code

    Use your phone's camera or the SmartAttend app. A code from another course or an expired code is refused.

  3. You prove it's you

    A short live face check, then your phone's fingerprint, Face ID or screen lock.

  4. You get a receipt

    Only after the server saves your record do you see "Present" with a reference number.

Why a classmate can't sign for you

Each check covers a different trick. All of them are verified on the server, never just on the phone.

Official register

Accounts can only be created for index numbers on the department's verified class list — one account per index number.

Live face check

You turn your head in a random order. A photo, a screenshot or a recorded video can't follow new instructions.

Your own phone

A passkey tied to your registered phone. Another student's phone can't sign for your account.

Rotating codes

Codes last 60 seconds and belong to one class session, so a code sent over WhatsApp expires before it's useful.

Classroom evidence

Classroom network and location are recorded as supporting evidence. Unusual results go to a lecturer for review, not automatic punishment.

Human review

Lost phone, failed camera, wrong record? Lecturers and administrators can verify you in person and correct records, with every change logged.
