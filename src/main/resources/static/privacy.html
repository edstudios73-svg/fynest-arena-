Privacy notice

Version 2026-11-v1 · Applies to the GCTU SmartAttend pilot. Prepared with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) in mind.

Who is responsible

Ghana Communication Technology University, Department of Computer Science, operates SmartAttend to record class attendance.

What we collect and why

  • Register details — index number, title and names from the official class register, to confirm who you are.
  • Face template — a 128-number mathematical description of your face, made from a live capture at enrolment. It is used only to check that the person marking attendance is you. Camera frames are analysed in memory and deleted immediately; photos are not stored.
  • Passkey public key — created by your phone. Your fingerprint or Face ID stays on your phone; we never receive it.
  • Attendance records — course, date, time, status and the checks that passed.
  • Security evidence — device identifier cookie, IP address, and location if you allow it, used only to detect misuse such as one phone signing in for several students.

What we don't do

  • We don't use your face template for anything except attendance verification at GCTU.
  • We don't search other databases or identify you in other photos.
  • We don't sell or share your data with advertisers.

How it is protected

  • Face templates are encrypted with AES-256-GCM; administrators can see only whether you're enrolled, never the template.
  • Connections use HTTPS. Data is encrypted in transit and the server encrypts biometric data at rest. The server must read attendance data to validate it, so this is not end-to-end encryption.
  • Every administrative change is written to an audit log that cannot be edited.

How long we keep it

Face templates are kept for the length of your programme (default 730 days) and then deleted. Attendance records follow the university's academic records policy.

Your choices

  • You can decline the face check and ask for supervised verification; your lecturer can then record your attendance in person.
  • You can ask the department to correct your register details or delete your face template.
  • A failed face check never permanently locks you out — a lecturer or administrator can verify you in person.

Contact

Contact the Department of Computer Science office for questions or requests about your data.
