About SmartAttend

SmartAttend is the attendance system for the Department of Computer Science at Ghana Communication Technology University. Its first pilot is the Diploma in Computer Science, Level 100 class, First Semester 2026/2027.

What it replaces

Paper registers are slow to pass around, easy to sign on behalf of an absent friend, and hard to total up at the end of the semester. SmartAttend records attendance as the lecture happens and calculates each student's percentage automatically.

Who uses it

  • Students register once with their index number, then mark attendance, check their timetable and follow their attendance percentage.
  • The admin opens attendance in class, shows the QR code and keeps the class list up to date.

Built to grow

Institutions, departments, programmes, levels and semesters are all configurable, so other classes can join without rebuilding the system.
